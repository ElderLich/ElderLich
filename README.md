I'm **ElderLich**. I mod Digimon games and maintain **MDPro4**, a community Yu-Gi-Oh! simulator. Give me a Digimon model and I want to see how it moves. Give me a Yu-Gi-Oh! card and I want to see the Lua that makes its effect work.

## Digimon modding

*Digimon Story Time Stranger* is the game I love taking apart. Two of my tools cover different sides of the same mod. One changes how a Digimon plays. The other gets its model and animations into Blender.

| On my workbench | What you can do with it |
| --- | --- |
| [DigiTS Helper](https://github.com/ElderLich/DigiTS-Helper) | Edit an evolution, change a skill or trait, and package the changes as a Reloaded II mod. |
| [Time Stranger Importer](https://github.com/ElderLich/Time-Stranger-Importer) | Open a `.geom` model with its textures and `.anim` clips in Blender, edit it, and export supported changes back to the game formats. |
| [Next Order Importer](https://github.com/ElderLich/Next-Order-Importer) | Bring *Digimon World: Next Order* models into Blender with their rigs and props. Beelzemon even gets to keep his motorcycle, Behemoth. |

## Yu-Gi-Oh!

I started out making small contributions to SherryChaos's MDPro3. At v1.4.0 I began taking on bigger changes, and I stayed with the team through the fork and the months of work that became MDPro4.

**Play MDPro4.** Try Progression Series with a friend. Open packs from the same set, build from different pulls and find out which cards are suddenly worth playing. [Get MDPro4 at the Workshop](https://github.com/ElderLich/MDPro4-Workshop#get-started) · [Browse the player guide](https://elderlich.github.io/MDPro4-FAQ/)

**Script a card.** The MDPro4 Sacred Book is my scripting reference. Start with the first-card guide, or look up a card you already know and read how its effect works. Standard and Rush Duel each have their own script collection. [Open the Sacred Book](https://elderlich.github.io/MDPro4_Sacred_Book/)
